Privacy Policy

Last updated July 24, 2026

This policy applies to all LucianoLabs apps, including Habits: Streaks & Routines, Countdown: Days Until, and our other iOS apps.

The short version: our apps keep all of your data on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, and there are no accounts, servers, analytics, or third-party trackers.

What we collect

Nothing. The habits, events, reminders, preferences, and any other content you create live only in the app's on-device storage (and, for widgets, a private app group shared between the app and its own widget on the same device). None of it leaves your device or is visible to us.

Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple may process purchase information under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device using Apple's notification system. They are not sent through any server of ours.

Children

Our apps do not collect data from anyone, including children, and are rated 4+.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and revise the date above.
