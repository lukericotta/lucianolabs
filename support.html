Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server — see our Privacy Policy.

I bought Pro on another device — how do I get it here?

Open the paywall and tap Restore Purchases. As long as you're signed in with the same Apple ID, your purchase will be restored.

My widget isn't updating.

Widgets refresh periodically and after you make changes in the app. If one looks stale, remove and re-add it from the Home Screen.

How do reminders work?

Reminders are scheduled locally through iOS notifications. Make sure notifications are enabled for the app in Settings.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Manage or cancel any subscription in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone.
